What this means

    Refill events

    Every time the tank gained water — when it started, how long it took, and how much came in.

    Emptying events

    Continuous draw-down stretches — useful for spotting leaks and heavy-use windows.

    Fill vs empty per interval

    Each bar is the change between two consecutive readings — above the line is water coming in, below is water going out.

    Biggest refills

    Biggest draw-downs

    Low-water episodes

    Periods where the level sat at or below your low alert threshold.

    Daily balance

    Water in vs water out for each day in range, with the closing level as a line.

    Activity split

    Total filled vs consumed vs what stayed in the tank.

    Usage rose

    Consumption grouped into 3-hour blocks around the clock.

    Running totals

    Cumulative water consumed vs filled across the window.

    Sensor distance

    Air gap between the sensor and the water surface — a sanity check on readings.

    Reporting gaps

    Minutes between uploads. Tall bars mean the device stayed quiet.
